Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026  ·  Effective: August 30, 2026

VyDigitalab ("we", "our", "us") built ZyntrAbsen as a B2B employee attendance management application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: Full name, work email address, and company information provided at registration.
  • Attendance records: Clock-in and clock-out timestamps, GPS coordinates, and address at the time of each record.
  • Overtime notes: Text notes you optionally add to overtime check-in records.
  • Device biometrics: Face ID / Touch ID is used only for local authentication. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Notification preferences: Overtime reminder settings stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To record and verify your attendance on behalf of your employer.
  • To calculate overtime hours and generate overtime reports.
  • To provide location verification for attendance records.
  • To send local notifications for overtime reminders (with your permission).

3. Data Sharing

Your attendance data is shared with your employer (company administrator) who manages your account. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to any third party.

We use Supabase as our backend infrastructure. Data is stored securely on Supabase servers. You can review Supabase's privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.

4. Data Retention

Attendance records are retained as long as your account is active in your company's workspace. When your account is removed by your company administrator, your data may be deleted according to your employer's data retention policy.

5. Location Data

ZyntrAbsen collects your GPS location only at the exact moment you clock in or clock out. Location is not tracked continuously. You may disable location access in your device Settings, but this will prevent location verification of attendance records.

6. Children's Privacy

ZyntrAbsen is intended for use by employees aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting your company administrator or reaching us at vyuliarto@gmail.com.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) to protect your data. No method of electronic transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:
